Refund Policy

All current features are free. This 14-day refund policy draft applies to existing BitDesktop Pro purchases. Pending formal legal review before commercial launch.

14-day refund rules

No purchase is needed for any current feature. Existing Pro purchases carry a 14-day refund window.

1. 14-day refund window

If you previously purchased a BitDesktop Pro license and it does not work well on your PC or meet your expectations, you can request a full refund within 14 days of purchase.

2. How refunds are processed

Payments and refunds are processed by Dodo Payments, our Merchant of Record. Use your Dodo Payments order confirmation receipt email within 14 days of purchase to request a refund.

3. After a refund

When a refund is issued, the Pro license key is deactivated. All current features, rooms, and local files in %LOCALAPPDATA%\BitDesktop remain intact.
