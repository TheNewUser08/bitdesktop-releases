Zero telemetry · Local Windows storage

Privacy Policy

BitDesktop stores your rooms, bookshelf pins, sticky notes, checklists, and photos locally in %LOCALAPPDATA%\BitDesktop with zero analytics or tracking SDKs.

Data and network requests

Room tools run offline. Connected calendar and mail accounts sync directly with Google or Microsoft after sign-in. Website builds can also check for updates and validate existing licenses.

1. Zero telemetry

BitDesktop does not collect usage analytics, crash telemetry, keystrokes, filenames, screenshots, or behavioral profiles. There are no third-party tracking SDKs in the Windows app or on this website.

2. Local data storage

Room layouts, bookshelf pins, folder assignments, sticky notes, focus sessions, custom sprites, and pasted corkboard photos stay locally on your PC in %LOCALAPPDATA%\BitDesktop. Exported .roompack files contain artwork, layout, and custom sprites only, never personal pins, notes, photos, or folder paths.

3. Daily update manifest check

Builds configured for update checks look for a newer version at most once a day. The download page checks the public GitHub Releases API for the current Windows downloads. These requests do not include hardware identifiers.

4. Pro license checks & Dodo Payments

All current features work without activating a license. If you choose to activate an existing Pro license, BitDesktop sends your license key and computer name to Dodo Payments, storing the key and activation ID in license.json on your PC. Daily validation lets Pro run offline for up to 14 days. Billing details are handled directly by Dodo Payments as Merchant of Record.

5. Optional calendar and mail connections

After you connect an account, BitDesktop reads your primary calendar and inbox directly from Google or Microsoft. It does not send mail or change events. Account tokens are protected with Windows DPAPI in accounts.dpapi; event and inbox summaries stay in memory. Messages you open in a mail app are saved as local .eml copies under mail-cache and cleared on the next app start or when that provider is disconnected or reconnected. Disconnecting removes the account from BitDesktop; you can also revoke its consent in your provider's connected-app settings.
